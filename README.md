# Supplementary information for the PhD thesis of Sofia Fraulini

This repository contains the supplementary materials associated with Chapters III and IV of the PhD thesis of Sofia Fraulini, developed at the Department of Biology, University of Padova.

## Repository contents

### Supplementary Information III

Supplementary methods, tables and figures associated with Chapter III, which describes the development and application of machine-learning models for predicting microbial functions relevant to anaerobic digestion.

### Supplementary Information IV

Supplementary methods, tables and figures associated with Chapter IV, which investigates microbial genome–environment associations at macro- and sub-environmental levels using sequence-derived and functional representations.

Supplementary materials associated with Chapters I and II are available with the corresponding published articles:

- Chapter I: https://doi.org/10.1186/s40793-024-00600-6
- Chapter II: https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210x.70413
